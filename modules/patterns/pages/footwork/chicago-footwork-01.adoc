== Character

A fast, two-bar original study whose kick placement changes often enough to invite a physical answer rather than establish a single four-beat formula. The repeating snare landmarks keep the loop legible while hats and percussion make the surface flicker.

== Rhythmic roles

Relay:: The kick is a sequence of short answers, not a bass drum ostinato.
Landmarks:: The snare provides a stable point of return across the two bars.
Pressure:: Bar two uses an accented first kick, then removes low-drum weight before the last response.

== Performance notes

* Begin at 160 BPM and reduce layers before reducing the tempo.
* Keep the kick dry and very short; let a separate bass part create longer movement.
* Make the clap in bar two an interruption, not a replacement for the snare.

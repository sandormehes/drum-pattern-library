== Pattern

[drum-pattern]
----
KICK   | X--- ---x ---- x--- | X--- ---- --x- ---x
SNARE  | ---- X--- ---- X--- | ---- X--- ---- X---
HI-HAT | x-x- x-x- x-x- x-x- | x-x- x-x- x-x- x-x-
SHAKER | --x- x--x --x- x--x | --x- x--x -x-- x--x
----

== Character

A two-bar 2-step study: the backbeat remains legible while the kick avoids a four-on-the-floor pattern and the shaker carries the shuffle. The second bar changes the kick path to prevent a one-bar mechanical loop.

== Groove setting

Swing:: Apply swing to hats and shaker, then judge the kicks by ear rather than shifting every lane equally.
Dynamics:: Alternate shaker strength and keep the snare more stable.

== Performance notes

* Keep low-end events short enough to leave room for a moving bass line.
* Avoid adding a kick to every quarter note.
* Try small vocal or percussion responses in the empty sixteenths without changing the foundation.

== Pattern

[drum-pattern]
----
KICK   | X--- ---x -x-- ---- | ---- x--- --x- -x--
SNARE  | ---- X--- ---- X--- | ---- X--- ---- X---
HAT    | x-x- xx-- -x-x x-x- | -x-x x-xx x-x- xx--
PERC A | --x- -x-- ---x x--- | x--- ---x -x-- --x-
PERC B | ---- x--g -x-- ---- | --g- x--- ---- x---
----

== Character

This original 140 BPM study keeps a grime-scale half-time backbeat while a pair of
percussion lanes pull sideways against it. Bar two moves the low-drum emphasis away
from the downbeat, making the returning snare feel heavier without adding density.

== Rhythmic roles

Frame:: The snare locks the two-bar span into a clear half-time reading.
Offset:: Kicks begin grounded, then enter bar two after beat one rather than on it.
Interlock:: PERC A supplies short dry replies while PERC B uses quieter, longer-feeling events to connect them.

== Performance notes

* Start with the snare and kick only; introduce percussion one lane at a time.
* Tune PERC A higher than PERC B so their separate rhythmic roles remain audible.
* Keep hats lower in the mix than the percussion; they should imply speed, not dominate the groove.

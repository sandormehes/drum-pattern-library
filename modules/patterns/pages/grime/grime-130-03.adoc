== Pattern

[source,text]
----
COUNT | 1e&a 2e&a 3e&a 4e&a
KICK  | X--- ---x --X- ----
SNARE | ---- X--- ---- X---
CHH   | x-x- x-x- x-x- x-x-
PERC  | --g- -x-- ---g x---
----

== Character

A half-time grime framework at 130 BPM. The firm kick accents and backbeat provide a stable outline while the percussion lane creates a quieter, asymmetrical counter-rhythm.

== Rhythmic roles

Foundation:: Kick and snare establish the large-scale pulse.
Subdivision:: Closed hats make the eighth-note grid audible.
Syncopation:: Normal and ghost percussion hits pull against the foundation without adding another strong accent.

== Performance notes

* Keep the ghost percussion clearly below the normal hits.
* Let the final percussion hit lead back into beat one.
* Try a metallic or short FM sound on the percussion lane without changing its rhythmic role.

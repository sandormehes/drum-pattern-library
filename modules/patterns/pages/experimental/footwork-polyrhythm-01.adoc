== Pattern

[drum-pattern]
----
KICK   | X--- --x- -x-- ---x | --x- -x-- ---- x---
SNARE  | ---- X--- ---- X--- | ---- X--- ---- X---
TOM    | --x- ---x -x-- ---- | -x-- --x- ---x -x--
HAT    | x-x- xx-x -x-x x-xx | xx-x -x-x x-xx -x-x
PERC   | -x-- x--- ---x x--g | x--- --x- x--g ----
----

== Character

An original fast-footwork study with a three-against-four feel implied by the tom
and percussion lanes. The fixed snare gives the pattern a readable centre while
the kick and tom figures keep changing their relationship to it across two bars.

== Design device

Cross-rhythm:: The tom gestures recur after uneven gaps, suggesting a cycle that is not confined to the bar.
Relay:: Kicks answer the tom attacks without duplicating their contour.
Anchor:: The snare remains fixed so the layered accents can be heard as tension rather than a meter change.

== Performance notes

* Choose a tom with a short, pitched body so its recurring contour is perceptible at speed.
* Keep the snare more consistent in velocity than the other lanes.
* Try muting the hats for one repeat; the kick-and-tom relationship should still carry the loop.

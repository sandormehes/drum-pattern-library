== Pattern

[drum-pattern]
----
KICK    | X--- X--- X--- X--- | X--- ---- X--- ----
SNARE   | ---- X--- ---- X--- | ---- X--- ---- ----
HI-HAT  | --x- --x- --x- --x- | --x- ---- --x- ----
OPEN HH | ---- --o- ---- --o- | ---- ---- ---- ---o
----

== Character

The first bar presents a full steppers foundation. The second removes alternate kicks, the final snare, and half the hats, treating subtraction as the variation rather than adding a fill.

== Transformations

Parent:: {parent-pattern}
Extension:: Expand the one-bar parent into a two-bar statement and response.
Dropout:: Remove selected foundation events in bar two.
Release:: Delay the final open hat until the last sixteenth of the cycle.

== Performance notes

* Change ambience or filter state when the sparse second bar arrives.
* Preserve the missing final snare; it creates the return to bar one.
* Keep the delayed open hat quieter than a conventional fill.

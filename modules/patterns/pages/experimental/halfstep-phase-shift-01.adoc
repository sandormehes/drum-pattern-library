== Pattern

[drum-pattern]
----
KICK   | X--- ---- ---x ---- | X--- --x- ---- ---x
SNARE  | ---- ---- X--- ---- | ---- ---X ---- ----
HI-HAT | --x- --x- --x- --x- | --x- --x- --x- --x-
PERC   | ---- -g-- ---- x--- | ---- x--- -g-- ----
----

== Character

Bar one states a familiar half-step snare on beat three. In bar two the accent arrives one sixteenth earlier, briefly destabilizing the frame before the loop resets.

== Design device

Expectation:: The first bar teaches the listener where the backbeat belongs.
Displacement:: The second backbeat moves from step 9 to step 8 within its bar.
Reset:: The opening kick restores the original phase every two bars.

== Performance notes

* Make the displaced snare unmistakably strong.
* Keep the hats steady so the shift reads as displacement, not loose timing.
* Leave a short silence immediately after the early snare.

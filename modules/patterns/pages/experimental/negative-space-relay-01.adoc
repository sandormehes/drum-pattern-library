== Character

No lane carries the groove alone. Events pass from kick to percussion to snare across two bars, creating continuity through handoff rather than density.

== Design device

Relay:: A new timbre answers the previous event instead of doubling it.
Asymmetry:: The only strong snares occur in different halves of the two bars.
Silence:: Empty quarter-note regions are structural and should remain exposed.

== Performance notes

* Give the final ghost percussion enough presence to point back toward beat one.
* Use related envelopes across the lanes so the handoffs feel intentional.
* Add ambience only if its decay does not erase the gaps.

== Pattern

[drum-pattern]
----
KICK   | X--x --x- -x-- x---
SNARE  | ---- X--- ---- X---
HI-HAT | --x- -x-- x--x --x-
PERC   | -x-- x--x --x- -x-x
----

== Character

Kick accents divide the 16-step bar into 3+3+3+3+4 while snare continues to mark beats two and four. Hats and percussion fill opposing parts of the cycle, producing one dense interlocking surface.

== Design device

Grouping:: Kick attacks begin groups of 3, 3, 3, 3, and 4 steps.
Contradiction:: The snare preserves 4/4 landmarks against the asymmetric grouping.
Interlock:: Hat and percussion placements are distributed to avoid simple unison.

== Performance notes

* Accent the first kick and slightly soften the remaining grouping markers.
* Assign contrasting pitches to percussion events instead of adding more hits.
* Mute the snare temporarily to hear the asymmetric grouping on its own.

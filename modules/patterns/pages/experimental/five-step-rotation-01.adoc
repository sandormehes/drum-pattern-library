== Pattern

[drum-pattern]
----
KICK   | X--- -x-- --x- ---x | ---- x--- -x-- --x-
SNARE  | ---- X--- ---- X--- | ---- X--- ---- X---
HI-HAT | x-x- x-x- x-x- x-x- | x-x- x-x- x-x- x-x-
PERC   | --x- ---g x--- -x-- | --g- x--- ---x -g--
----

== Character

The kick repeats every five sixteenth notes while the snare states ordinary 4/4. Their alignment keeps changing, producing a cross-cycle without changing meter.

== Design device

Rotation:: Kick attacks fall on steps 1, 6, 11, 16, 21, 26, and 31.
Anchor:: The backbeat prevents the rotating kick from obscuring the bar line.
Counterline:: Percussion avoids most kick positions and gives the cycle a second contour.

== Performance notes

* Keep the first kick accented; otherwise let the five-step cycle feel even.
* Do not swing the kick lane. Swinging hats or percussion is optional.
* Try alternating two kick pitches to make the rotation easier to hear.

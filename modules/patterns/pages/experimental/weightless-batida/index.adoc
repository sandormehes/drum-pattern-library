= Weightless × Batida Experiments

This series explores one deliberately speculative meeting point: the spatially untethered, tension-heavy club architecture associated with Logos, Mumdance, and Different Circles, crossed with fast, stripped, interlocking percussion informed by Lisbon batida.

These are original composition studies. “Weightless” describes the treatment of space, impact, and expectation; “batida-informed” describes percussion-led motion and interlock. Neither term is used to claim authorship, authenticity, or a transcription of the referenced artists.

== Series constraints

* Work around 136–142 BPM.
* Let percussion carry motion while kicks and snares remain discontinuous.
* Treat silence, tails, and isolated metallic events as parts of the rhythm.
* Document the device that distinguishes each pattern.
* Keep regional context specific: this series refers primarily to the Afro-diasporic Lisbon batida scene documented in the xref:research:sources.adoc#src-principe-profile[Príncipe profile].

== Patterns

* xref:experimental/weightless-batida/vacuum-batida-01.adoc[Vacuum Batida 01] — dense percussion suspended around a nearly absent drum foundation.
* xref:experimental/weightless-batida/ping-interlock-01.adoc[Ping Interlock 01] — isolated metallic signals threaded through an interlocking percussion cell.
* xref:experimental/weightless-batida/off-one-pressure-01.adoc[Off-One Pressure 01] — a two-bar groove that refuses a kick on either downbeat.
* xref:experimental/weightless-batida/locked-groove-erasure-01.adoc[Locked-Groove Erasure 01] — a propulsive first bar progressively erased in the second.
* xref:experimental/weightless-batida/seven-step-bell-01.adoc[Seven-Step Bell Rotation 01] — a seven-sixteenth bell cycle passing across 4/4.
* xref:experimental/weightless-batida/cold-percussion-relay-01.adoc[Cold Percussion Relay 01] — percussion roles handed between dry, metallic, and sustained voices.

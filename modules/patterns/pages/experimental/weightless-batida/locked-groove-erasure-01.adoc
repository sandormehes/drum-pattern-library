== Character

Bar one behaves like a compact percussion lock. Bar two removes most of that information, leaving isolated memories of the original pattern and one suspended metal tone.

== Design device

Statement:: The first bar establishes a dense, repeating physical pattern.
Erasure:: The second bar retains only selected structural residues.
Memory:: Surviving hits occupy positions heard in the first bar, so absence becomes legible.

== Performance notes

* Keep identical sounds and levels for events that survive into bar two.
* Automate space or noise upward as event density falls.
* Avoid a turnaround fill; the erased bar is already the variation.

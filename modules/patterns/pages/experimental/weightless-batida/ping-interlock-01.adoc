== Pattern

[drum-pattern]
----
KICK   | X--- ---- --x- ----
SNARE  | ---- ---- X--- ----
PING   | --x- x--- ---x -x--
PERC   | -x-x --x- x--- --x-
SHAKER | ---- -g-- ---- -g--
----

== Character

Four isolated pings form a cold signal path while the percussion fills different positions around them. The half-step foundation remains recognizable but does not dominate the surface.

== Design device

Interlock:: Ping and percussion never strike together.
Signal:: One bright, narrow-band sound creates identity with only four events.
Restraint:: Ghost shakers suggest subdivision without a continuous hat line.

== Performance notes

* Tune each ping identically and vary decay rather than pitch.
* Keep the percussion dry and close; place the ping in a longer artificial space.
* Try muting the snare every fourth repetition without adding a fill.

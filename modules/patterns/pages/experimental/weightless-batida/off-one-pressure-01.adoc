== Pattern

[drum-pattern]
----
KICK   | ---x ---- --x- ---- | -x-- ---- ---- x---
SNARE  | ---- ---- X--- ---- | ---- ---X ---- ----
PERC   | x-x- -x-x x--x -x-- | --x- x-x- -xx- x--x
TOM    | ---- x--- ---- --x- | x--- ---- -x-- ----
METAL  | ---- ---- ---o ---- | ---- x--- ---- ----
----

== Character

Neither bar begins with a kick. Percussion and toms announce the downbeats indirectly, while a late first-bar snare and early second-bar snare keep the two-bar frame unstable.

== Design device

Refusal:: Both downbeats are left without a low-frequency foundation hit.
Pressure:: The dense upper percussion makes each absent kick conspicuous.
Drift:: The main snare moves one sixteenth earlier in bar two.

== Performance notes

* Do not rescue the bar line with bass notes on beat one.
* Use a low tom that is clearly lighter than the kick.
* Let the single sustained metal hit mark the midpoint, not a conventional crash.

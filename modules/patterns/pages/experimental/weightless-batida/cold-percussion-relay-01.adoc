== Character

Dry wood and metal voices exchange a nearly continuous rhythm while sustained tones appear only at the end of each phrase. The foundation is sparse enough that the changing material of the relay becomes the groove.

== Design device

Relay:: Wood and metal alternate roles without simple call-and-response symmetry.
Temperature:: Dry wooden attacks contrast with cold, narrow metallic hits.
Afterimage:: Sustained events occupy gaps after the busiest exchanges.

== Performance notes

* Make wood and metal equally present but spectrally distinct.
* Pan relay voices slightly apart; keep kick and snare centered.
* Do not quantize the sustained tails—shape their endings by ear.

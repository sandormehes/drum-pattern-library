== Character

This original study uses a rotating clave-like lane as the source of motion, with
kick attacks appearing only when that lane leaves space. The result is a fast,
angular footwork framework whose percussion changes the listener's sense of where
the next answer will land.

== Rhythmic roles

Rotor:: CLAVE moves its attack contour forward at the bar line rather than repeating exactly.
Negative space:: Kicks occupy selected openings in the rotor, preventing either lane from becoming a continuous pulse.
Flash:: Brief hats and ghost percussion make the sixteenth grid audible without resolving its asymmetry.

== Performance notes

* Use a woodblock, rim, or synthetic click for CLAVE; clarity matters more than realism.
* Let the first kick of the loop be the loudest low-drum attack, then keep subsequent kicks compact.
* If adding bass, answer only two or three of the kick hits per cycle to preserve the space.

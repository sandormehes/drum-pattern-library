== Pattern

[drum-pattern]
----
KICK   | X--- ---- -x-- ---- | ---- x--- ---- --x-
SNARE  | ---- X--- ---- ---- | X--- ---- ---- X---
BELL   | --x- ---x -x-- --x- | -x-- --x- ---x -x--
PERC A | -x-- x--- ---x x--- | --x- x--- x--- ---x
PERC B | ---- --g- x--- --x- | x--- -x-- --g- x---
----

== Character

An original, percussion-led two-bar study built around a turning bell phrase and
short answer figures. The low drum stays deliberately discontinuous, leaving the
interlocking upper voices to create the forward motion.

== Rhythmic roles

Bell:: A five-event contour shifts its placement in bar two without becoming a fixed metronome.
Answers:: The two percussion lanes take alternating spaces around the bell.
Weight:: Sparse kick and snare events give the loop large landmarks but do not flatten its motion into a standard backbeat.

== Performance notes

* Use a dry, short bell tone with a distinct pitch from both percussion lanes.
* Keep PERC B's ghost hits notably quieter than its normal attacks.
* On a later repeat, remove the last bar-two kick and let the bell phrase pull the loop back to the start.

== Character

A sparse 140 BPM half-step framework with its main snare on beat three. The apparent slowness of the drum frame leaves room for sub-bass and small syncopated events at the full tempo.

== Performance notes

* Give the beat-three snare enough weight to define the entire bar.
* Keep the late percussion distinct from the sub-bass rhythm.
* Use delay or reverb as rhythmic events, with space preserved around their tails.

== Character

A compact drum-machine study of early digital dancehall economy. The extra percussion supplies the circular motion while the kick and snare remain deliberately plain.

== Performance notes

* Use short, synthetic sounds and avoid acoustic-room realism.
* Keep the final ghost hit quiet enough to be felt as a pickup.
* Pair the drums with an independent bass motif rather than copying the kick lane.

== Pattern

[drum-pattern]
----
KICK   | ---- ---- X--- ----
RIM    | ---- ---- X--- ----
HI-HAT | x-x- x-x- x-x- x-x-
----

== Character

An intentionally spare educational reduction of a one-drop feel. Beat three carries the joined kick-and-rim weight while the hi-hat keeps the underlying pulse audible.

== Performance notes

* Let beat one breathe; do not add a kick simply because the bar feels empty.
* Play the rim with a dry cross-stick articulation.
* Introduce small hi-hat dynamics only after the placement of beat three feels settled.

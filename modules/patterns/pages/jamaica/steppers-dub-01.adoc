== Pattern

[drum-pattern]
----
KICK    | X--- X--- X--- X---
SNARE   | ---- X--- ---- X---
HI-HAT  | --x- --x- --x- --x-
OPEN HH | ---- --o- ---- --o-
----

== Character

A simple steppers foundation: kick on every quarter note, backbeat on two and four, and hats emphasizing the offbeats.

== Performance notes

* Keep the quarter-note kick steady but avoid making every hit identical.
* Use a roomy snare or rim sound; the pattern itself should remain uncluttered.
* Treat the open hats as optional color rather than the main pulse.

== Character

A two-bar programmed breakbeat study. The first bar states a stable kick-and-snare frame; the second redistributes kicks and adds a ghost snare so the loop turns over rather than merely repeating.

== Performance notes

* Treat each lane as the result of slicing and rearranging a break, even when programming single hits.
* Keep the backbeat strong while allowing the surrounding events to change level and tone.
* Test the pattern with substantial sub-bass and open space between phrases.

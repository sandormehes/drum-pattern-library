== Pattern

[drum-pattern]
----
KICK   | X--- --x- ---- x--x
SNARE  | ---- X--- ---- X---
HI-HAT | x-xx x-xx x-xx xxox
PERC   | --xg x--- -xg- x--g
----

== Character

A 140 BPM half-time grime foundation activated by clipped, interlocking percussion.
The kick and snare retain a firm UK framework, while the percussion lane suggests
a batida-informed cycle that keeps moving through the gaps rather than filling them.

== Rhythmic roles

Foundation:: The accented first kick and fixed half-time snare establish the grime backbone.
Subdivision:: Dense closed hats give motion without turning into a straight trap roll.
Syncopation:: Percussion alternates normal and ghost hits, creating a separate dancing layer.
Release:: The late kick pair and open hat create momentum into the loop point.

== Groove setting

Swing:: Apply approximately 55% swing to the hi-hat and percussion lanes only.
Snare timing:: Keep both snares essentially quantized and forceful.
Microtiming:: Nudge selected ghost percussion slightly late; allow one normal percussion hit per cycle to arrive slightly early.
Dynamics:: Keep ghost hits markedly quieter than the normal percussion. The open hat is a release, not an accent.

== Performance notes

* Use dry, synthetic drums: a compact kick, clipped snare, and metallic or woody percussion.
* Do not add extra snares to fill the space—the half-time weight is essential.
* Let the percussion change timbre or pitch between repetitions while preserving the event pattern.
* A bass phrase should answer the late kicks rather than double every kick.

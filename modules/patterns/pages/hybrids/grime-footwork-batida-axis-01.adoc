== Pattern

[drum-pattern]
----
KICK   | X--- -x-- ---x --x- | ---- x--- -x-- x---
SNARE  | ---- X--- ---- X--- | ---- ---- X--- ----
BELL   | x--- ---x --x- ---- | -x-- ---- x--- ---x
HAT    | -x-x x-xx -x-x xx-x | x-xx -x-x xxx- -x-x
PERC   | --x- x--- -x-g x--- | x--g --x- -x-- x-g-
----

== Character

A stable grime half-time snare and a turning bell figure establish two different axes.
The kick sequence changes its landing points across both bars, while the percussion
threads the remaining gaps into a clipped, forward-moving dance layer.

== Design device

Backbeat:: The snare makes the fast tempo read in half-time.
Rotation:: The bell is deliberately offset between bars, so it does not function as a fixed clap pattern.
Displacement:: Kicks avoid a four-beat cycle and change their contour after the bar line.
Interlock:: Percussion avoids direct unison with bell and kick attacks wherever possible.

== Groove setting

Timing:: Quantize the bell tightly; nudge only ghost percussion late.
Dynamics:: Let the bell speak sharply but briefly. Keep its level below the snare.
Variation:: On every fourth repeat, omit the final bell hit and let the pattern restart exposed.

== Performance notes

* A sine sub or short bass stab can answer the first and third kick attacks in each bar.
* Use two contrasting percussion pitches to make the interlock easier to hear.
* Avoid adding a second continuous shaker—the hats already provide the upper-grid glue.

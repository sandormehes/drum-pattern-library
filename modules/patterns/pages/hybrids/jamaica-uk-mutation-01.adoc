== Character

A controlled mutation of xref:jamaica/sparse-dancehall-01.adoc[Sparse Dancehall 01]. Removing the second backbeat creates a large pocket of negative space, while the rotated percussion lane and pickup kick push the loop toward a UK half-time feel.

== Transformations

Parent:: {parent-pattern}
Backbeat:: Remove the snare on beat four.
Kick:: Remove the late beat-two kick and add a sixteenth-note pickup before the loop repeats.
Percussion:: Rotate and simplify the syncopation; turn the second event into a ghost hit.
Tempo:: Raise the working range from roughly 100 BPM to roughly 130 BPM.

== Performance notes

* Resist filling the missing second backbeat; the absence is the main mutation.
* Keep the pickup kick lighter than beat one.
* Compare Jamaican digital percussion and metallic UK electronic timbres using the same event pattern.
